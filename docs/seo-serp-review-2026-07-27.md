## Gap diagnosis and action

1. **Proof:** specialist results make their credibility legible with named clients, numbers, partnerships or detailed case context. Go Marketing must not imitate this with unsupported claims; the existing evidence intake remains the publication gate.
2. **Process:** buyers can see setup, content, community and measurement steps before contacting several competitors. The planned comparison guide should explain operational prerequisites and handoffs, not merely platform differences.
3. **Commercial clarity:** at least one specialist publishes price ranges. Go Marketing does not need to copy competitor pricing, but the guide should identify the factors that change scope and state what a useful discovery review will decide.
4. **Sector specificity:** hospitality, property, healthcare/wellness and professional services recur. The support guide should use concrete Sydney scenarios while avoiding fabricated outcomes.
5. **Ownership:** the Chinese agency page owns agency-selection intent; the Chinese community page owns broader local audience growth; the new guide must stay educational and comparative.
