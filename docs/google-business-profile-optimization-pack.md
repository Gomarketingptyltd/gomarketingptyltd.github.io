# Google Business Profile Optimization Pack

This pack is designed to turn GBP work into a short, practical update pass rather than a vague marketing task.

## What Google says to keep in mind

Based on current Google help documentation:

- Choose one **specific primary category** that best matches what the business does, and you can add up to **9 more categories**.
- The business description should focus on what the business offers, what makes it special and anything helpful for customers to know.
- Do **not** include URLs or HTML in the description.
- Keep the description under **750 characters**.
- Service businesses can add service categories, suggested services and custom services in the services editor.
- Custom services should stay clean and factual. Avoid phone numbers, prices inside names or anything that reads like spam.

Official references:

- [Edit your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617?hl=en)
- [Manage your Business Profile description](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13682007?hl=en&p=web_bizgendescription)
- [Manage your services on your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9455399?hl=en)

## Recommended update order

1. Primary category and additional categories
2. Business description
3. Services editor
4. Website and social links
5. Photos
6. Optional Q&A / profile posts

## Category guidance

Google's available category list can change, so use the closest available option in the category picker.

Recommended order to test:

1. `Marketing agency` as the primary category if it is available and fits your profile
2. `Advertising agency`
3. `Internet marketing service`
4. `Marketing consultant`
5. `Website designer`

Use only the categories that genuinely match the work. Do not fill all fields just to add keywords.

- Low-resolution graphics
