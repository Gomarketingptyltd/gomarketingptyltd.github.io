# SEO Case Study Brief: Chinese-Audience Growth in Sydney

Date: 2026-07-13

## Purpose

Create one evidence-led case page that supports the `chinese marketing agency sydney` owner page and the `chinese marketing sydney` service page. The asset must demonstrate how Go Marketing makes cross-market and platform decisions; it must not become generic agency copy or claim results that cannot be verified.

## Proposed page

- Working URL: `/services/caseStudyChineseAudienceGrowthSydney.html`
- Primary role: commercial proof for `services/sydneyBilingualMarketingAgency.html`
- Secondary role: contextual support for `services/chineseCommunityGrowth.html`
- Search intent: buyers comparing a Chinese or bilingual marketing agency in Sydney and looking for evidence of relevant work
- Indexing decision: do not publish or request indexing until the evidence gate below is complete

## Evidence gate

Obtain written internal/client approval for every publishable fact:

- client name, or an approved anonymised description such as “Sydney hospitality group”
- sector, Sydney market and project dates
- starting business problem and baseline
- channels actually used: website, Google, Xiaohongshu, WeChat, paid media, email or CRM
- deliverables actually completed
- at least one verified outcome with a source and measurement window
- permission for logo, screenshot, quote and campaign creative use
- any required redaction or confidentiality wording

Do not substitute estimates, vanity metrics or unsupported percentages. If commercial results cannot be disclosed, use verifiable process evidence: approved before/after messaging, published assets, response-time improvement, qualified-enquiry examples or documented stakeholder outcomes.

## Recommended narrative

1. **Client context** — sector, Sydney audience and why Chinese-speaking and mainstream Australian communication both mattered.
2. **The trust gap** — what customers could not understand or believe before the work.
3. **Audience and platform diagnosis** — why each selected channel had a different role.
4. **What changed** — bilingual message, landing page, Xiaohongshu/WeChat direction, search, content or follow-up workflow.
5. **Evidence** — a small table with baseline, action, outcome, source and dates.
6. **What the result means** — explain the commercial lesson without overstating attribution.
7. **Relevant service paths** — links to the agency owner page and Chinese Community Growth page.

## Required proof modules

- One approved client or anonymised project summary above the fold
- One before/after message or journey example
- One platform-role diagram or short table grounded in the actual project
- One verified outcome block with measurement dates
- One approved quote, or an explicit note that no testimonial is available
- One “constraints and what we learned” section to make the case credible and useful
