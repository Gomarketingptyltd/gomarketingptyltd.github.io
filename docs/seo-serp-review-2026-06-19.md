# SERP Review

Date: 2026-06-19

## Purpose

This review adds an external market lens to the Search Console workflow. GSC tells us how gomarketing.net.au is performing; SERP review tells us what Google is currently rewarding for the target queries.

Use this as a tactical input, not as a blind copy plan.

## Current competitor pattern

| Query family | Visible competitor examples | What Google appears to reward | Implication for Go Marketing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `marketing agency sydney` / `digital marketing services sydney` | Sydney Digital Marketing, The Collective Co, Sparro, Digital Nomads HQ, RGC Digital Marketing, agency directories such as Clutch and Semrush | Clear Sydney positioning, broad service coverage, proof/reviews/directories, lead-generation framing | Go Marketing needs sharper Sydney proof, services summary, case-style evidence, and differentiated multicultural positioning on owner pages |
| `chinese marketing agency sydney` / `chinese marketing agency australia` | Ignite Search Chinese marketing page, Brand Asia, Vantage Digital, BE BOLDER, S&J Media Group | China/Chinese-market specialisation, bilingual/localisation credibility, platform knowledge, years/clients/industry proof | `sydneyBilingualMarketingAgency.html` needs stronger proof blocks and a clearer "not translation, but market-entry strategy" angle |
| `xiaohongshu marketing sydney` / `xiaohongshu marketing australia` | Brand Asia, OZClicks, VisionVivo, Trafficon, LOC'X, Xiaohongshu/RedNote explainers | Platform-specific expertise, influencer/UGC language, Australia-China bridge, category examples such as restaurants, beauty, wellness, property | `chineseCommunityGrowth.html` and the restaurant support article should lean into local Sydney scenarios and platform-specific execution details |
| `marketing support services` | Mixed intent: business support, managed marketing help, agency support, consulting | Search intent is less clean; pages that clarify scope fast have an advantage | `support.html` should keep positioning as a practical review and implementation support page, not a generic marketing page |

## Tactical content gaps

| Gap | Priority | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More concrete proof for Chinese-market work | High | Add compact examples, mini-case patterns, industries served, and process details to the Chinese agency owner page |
| Sydney-specific digital services proof | High | Add "for Sydney small/service businesses" examples to `digital.html` and its next support article |
| Xiaohongshu restaurant/local-services depth | High | Expand the restaurant article into a more practical playbook if impressions appear |
| Third-party trust and local citations | Medium | Strengthen Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Australian directories, partner mentions, and case-study pages |
| Competitor title/meta monitoring | Medium | Add a monthly manual SERP title review for the four priority query families |

## Next SERP review rules

- Run a SERP review every Monday before choosing the week's strongest push.
- Do not copy competitor page structure blindly; identify the missing trust signal or intent coverage.
- If Go Marketing lacks proof against the ranking pages, prefer case-study/support content over another title rewrite.
- If competitors are directories, reinforce third-party trust signals instead of over-optimising service copy.
