## SERP-prioritised evidence packet — 2026-08-10

Current Sydney Chinese-marketing and Xiaohongshu results commonly show local sector context, named channel roles, a delivery process and quantified outcomes. To close that gap without copying competitors or inventing proof, collect one complete Priority A packet before filling lower-value fields.

### Priority A — minimum packet that unlocks drafting

| Evidence needed | Exact source to attach | Why a buyer needs it | Ready? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One real Sydney sector and buyer problem | Approved brief, proposal or client email naming the sector, audience and original problem | Establishes local and commercial relevance |  |
| One dated baseline and one dated outcome | Analytics/CRM/platform export with the same metric definition and comparison window | Makes the result verifiable rather than promotional |  |
| Channel-role sequence | Approved campaign plan or project record showing what Xiaohongshu, WeChat, search and the website each did | Demonstrates strategy beyond translation or disconnected posting |  |
| One before/after work sample | Approved, redacted screenshot or copy example with dates | Lets buyers see the practical change |  |
| Publication permission | Written approval naming allowed client descriptor, claims, assets and redactions | Prevents confidentiality and attribution risk |  |

### Priority B — add only after Priority A is complete

- A client quotation tied to the documented problem or outcome.
- A three-to-five-step delivery timeline with real dates and owners.
- A sector-specific lesson that another Sydney buyer can apply.
- A limitation or attribution caveat explaining what the project did not prove.

Do not treat follower totals, reach or impressions alone as the commercial outcome. If enquiry or revenue data is unavailable, use an approved operational outcome such as a corrected customer journey, a measurable increase in qualified actions, or a documented reduction in response friction, with the evidence source and caveat attached.
