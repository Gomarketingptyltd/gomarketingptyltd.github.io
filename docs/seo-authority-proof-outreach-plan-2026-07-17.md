## Evidence request

Send the project owner a link or copy of the evidence intake and request only these five items first:

1. approved public client name or anonymised Sydney sector description;
2. the business problem and dated baseline source;
3. the channels and deliverables Go Marketing actually completed;
4. one verifiable outcome with measurement dates and an attribution caveat;
5. written permission for the selected screenshots, quotation and public wording.

If no measurable commercial outcome can be disclosed, use approved process evidence such as a before/after message, published campaign asset, documented enquiry example or stakeholder outcome. Do not convert an estimate into a public metric.

## Authority placement rules

- Prefer a relevant client, partner, association or local business profile over a generic directory.
- Ask for the Go Marketing brand name or a natural description such as `bilingual marketing partner in Sydney`; do not prescribe exact-match anchors repeatedly.
- Link to the future case page when the mention describes the project. Link to `https://gomarketing.net.au/services/sydneyBilingualMarketingAgency.html` when it describes agency capability.
- Reject paid bulk-link packages, unrelated guest-post networks and placements that cannot identify a real editorial or business relationship.
- Record the live URL, link target, anchor/context, owner and verification date before counting the signal as complete.
