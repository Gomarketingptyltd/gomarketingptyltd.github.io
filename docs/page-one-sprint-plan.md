## Fastest safe sprint cycle

### Week 1

- Tighten titles, descriptions and intro paragraphs on the top opportunity pages.
- Strengthen internal links from ranking pages to unindexed or weaker service pages.
- Re-submit priority URLs through Search Console URL Inspection.

### Week 2

- Check which queries and pages gained impressions or improved positions.
- Refine only the pages that are already moving.
- Do not add broad new blog content unless a real query gap appears.

### Week 3

- Strengthen click intent on any page sitting around positions 8 to 25.
- Tighten FAQ wording and first-screen copy around the real search query.
- Re-check the two weaker service pages that were previously discovered but not indexed.

### Week 4

- Compare performance against the baseline:
  - impressions
  - clicks
  - CTR
  - average position
- Keep pushing only the pages that are still gaining traction.

## Manual guidance cadence

The best rhythm is:

- manual guidance every 7 days
- or sooner only when one of these happens:
  - a major new page is published
  - Search Console shows a sharp rise on a new query
  - Google-side actions are needed, such as Request Indexing or Business Profile updates

For most weeks, one Monday review is enough.

## What we should not do during this sprint

- Do not publish lots of glossary or definition articles.
- Do not redesign the homepage again.
- Do not change many pages at once without fresh Search Console data.
- Do not chase low-intent topics that are not tied to service demand.
