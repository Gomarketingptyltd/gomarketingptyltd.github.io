Before we begin

A few useful answers.

Clear essentials now. A recommendation tailored to your business when we talk.

Do I need my own Xiaohongshu account?

For account management, we work through your business's account and you retain ownership. For a feature on GG Takes You Around Sydney, we publish through our channel, so you do not need to manage a separate account.

Can you use our existing photos and videos?

Yes. We recommend 3–10 well-chosen images per photo post, rather than treating this as a strict platform limit. Short, focused videos are preferred. A promotional or educational video may run up to two minutes where the content justifies it; we do not guarantee that viewers will watch it through. New photography, filming and advanced editing require a separate scope.

Can I review the content before publication?

Yes. You review the draft and confirm business details, any promotion conditions and the proposed publishing arrangements. We publish only after approval.

What if we need changes or are late with feedback?

Account-management content follows your brief or an agreed plan, with up to three revision rounds per post. If you are late providing materials or feedback, we can agree a revised schedule without automatically reducing the monthly fee. Feedback deadlines and any carryover are agreed before starting. No reply is not approval: unapproved content is not automatically published. A new direction or additional work needs a separately agreed scope. The client-delay rule is not a blanket no-refund policy and does not determine remedies for work we have not delivered as agreed.

Is this a one-off feature or ongoing support?

Channel promotion can be discussed as a single feature or a planned series. Account management is an ongoing service with an agreed content volume and fixed contract term. The schedule and commitment are confirmed in writing before you proceed.

Are reports and customer replies included?

Every package published through our channel includes a basic performance report, including the entry listing. The default is around two weeks after publication; a different time can be agreed at your request. Account-management reports and handling messages or comments on your own account remain separate options. A report is not an outcome guarantee or an unlimited reporting service.

Can I upgrade an Everyday Dining listing?

Yes. An upgrade within three months can include the original listing in the three- or five-post total: one published listing leaves two or four further posts. We confirm the remaining schedule and private quotation before the upgrade; these are not three or five extra posts.

How is my quotation prepared?

We consider the publishing account, number and type of posts, available assets, timing, production needs and any additional support. You receive a written scope and quotation before work starts, with optional items identified separately.

What results should I expect?

Premium includes paid platform advertising. Your written proposal confirms the target, platform metric, assessment window and shortfall arrangements before work starts. Other plans do not automatically include paid distribution. Views or impressions are not unique customers, and no plan guarantees enquiries, bookings, sales or complete video viewing.

Can we discuss relevant examples privately?

Where permission allows, we can discuss selected examples during a consultation. Client identities, confidential project details and protected materials are not shared without appropriate authorisation.
