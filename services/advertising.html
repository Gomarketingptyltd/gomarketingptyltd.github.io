FAQ

Common questions about advertising support

These questions usually help businesses decide whether the next move should be better ads, better landing pages, or clearer cross-market communication before more spend goes live.

What does advertising support usually include?

It can include audience review, paid-search and paid-social direction, landing-page clarity, campaign messaging, Chinese-community platform context and the follow-up path that turns visibility into enquiries.

Should we run ads before the website messaging is clear?

Usually no. If the service offer, proof points and next step still feel vague, paid traffic will reach confusion faster. Stronger landing-page clarity often improves campaign quality before more spend is added.

Can one campaign approach work for both Chinese-speaking and mainstream audiences?

Sometimes, but often not without adjustment. The same business may need different hooks, proof points, examples or platform choices for Chinese-Australian audiences and mainstream Australian audiences.

Do you only help with paid ads?

No. We look at the whole visibility path, including audience fit, website messaging, community-platform logic and what happens after someone clicks or enquires.
