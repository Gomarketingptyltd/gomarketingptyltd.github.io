Common questions

Useful answers before we talk

Do I need my own Xiaohongshu account?

For account management, we work through your business account and you retain ownership. For a feature on GG Takes You Around Sydney, we publish through our channel, so you do not need to manage a separate account.

How much does Xiaohongshu marketing cost?

Please contact us for pricing. Your quotation depends on the publishing account, number and type of posts, available assets, timing, production needs and optional support. You receive a written scope and quotation before work begins.

Are reports and customer replies included?

Every package published through our channel includes a basic performance report, normally around two weeks after publication unless another time is requested and agreed. Reports, meetings and handling messages or comments on your own account are separate management options, not automatic inclusions or 24/7 support.

What if we need revisions or are late with feedback?

Account management includes up to three revision rounds per post within the agreed brief. Client delays can be handled with an agreed revised schedule without automatically reducing the monthly fee. Deadlines and carryover are agreed before starting. Silence is not approval, and unapproved content is not automatically published. A new direction or extra work needs a separate scope.

Can you use our existing photos and videos?

Yes. We recommend 3-10 well-chosen images per photo post, not as a platform limit. Short, focused videos are preferred; promotional or educational content may run up to two minutes where justified. Complete viewing is not guaranteed. New photography, filming and advanced editing require a separate scope.

What results can you promise?

Premium includes paid platform advertising. Campaign targets, platform metrics, assessment windows and shortfall arrangements are confirmed in the written proposal. Other plans do not automatically include paid distribution. Views and impressions are not unique customers, and no plan guarantees enquiries, bookings or sales.

Can we see relevant examples?

Where permission allows, we can discuss selected examples privately during a consultation. Client identities, confidential project details and protected materials are not shared without appropriate authorisation. Historical performance is not a forecast for your campaign.
