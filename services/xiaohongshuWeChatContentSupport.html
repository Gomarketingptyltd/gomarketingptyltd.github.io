This service is for Sydney businesses that know Chinese-community platforms matter, but need a clearer plan for what Xiaohongshu and WeChat should each do, what kind of content fits and how those touchpoints connect to the wider website and customer journey.

Platform roles

The value comes from matching the platform to the decision journey

01

Xiaohongshu for discovery

Useful when people are searching for recommendations, local experiences, service comparisons or lifestyle context before they know the brand directly.

02

WeChat for relationship

Useful when the business already has an audience, a community, repeat customers or a reason to keep people updated after the first contact.

03

Website for credibility

Useful when platform content needs a stronger place to send people, confirm details and support a more professional next step.
