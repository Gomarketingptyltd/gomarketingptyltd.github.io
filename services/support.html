FAQ

Common questions before you contact us

These questions usually help businesses work out whether they need a full service project, a clearer first review, or a more focused support path around messaging, automation or campaign direction.

What do marketing support services usually include?

Common support areas include bilingual messaging review, Chinese-community communication, landing-page clarity, campaign direction, automation flow and practical advice on where marketing activity should start.

Can you help with both Chinese-speaking and mainstream Australian audiences?

Yes. That cross-market layer is a core part of the work, especially where trust, audience fit, landing-page logic and message clarity need to hold across both contexts.

Do we need a full brief before getting in touch?

No. A short explanation of the business, sector, current marketing challenge and any existing website or campaign links is enough to begin. We can help clarify the next step from there.

Can you review our current website, campaigns or messaging first?

Yes. That is often the most practical starting point when the business already has activity in market, but the website, paid traffic or platform content still feels too broad, unclear or uneven across channels.
