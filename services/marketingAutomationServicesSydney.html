FAQ

Common questions about marketing automation services in Sydney

Can Go Marketing build an app and help with app-store launch?

Yes. Business app development and app-store launch support are part of our services. We first confirm who will use the app and what they need to do, then agree the platform, features, testing, submission materials and account responsibilities. App-store approval and review timing are controlled by the platform, not guaranteed by Go Marketing. Release support and ongoing maintenance are scoped separately.

Can you connect our existing website and tools?

We assess your current setup before proposing a rebuild. Connections depend on the access, APIs and permissions available in each tool. Existing forms and customer records are not migrated without an agreed scope and testing plan.

Can automation still work when the business serves Chinese-speaking and English-speaking audiences?

Yes. We can plan Chinese and English content, routing and follow-up around each audience, with agreed content review and human handover. The languages, channels and customer questions to cover are confirmed during scoping; one translated script is not assumed to fit every customer.

Should automation come before ads or before more SEO?

If the business is already getting enquiries but handling them poorly, automation can be a higher priority than adding more traffic. If the message is still unclear, website and service-page clarity often need attention first.

What is agreed before a build starts?

Features, data access, account and code ownership, testing, handover and ongoing support are agreed in writing. Hosting, software subscriptions and third-party charges are clarified separately. A tailored system does not automatically mean every component is exclusively owned by the client. Automated customer replies use approved information and need a route to a person.

Can you tailor a CRM or internal system to our company?

Yes. Go Marketing can build company-specific CRM and marketing workflows or improve an existing system. We start with the tasks, users, handovers and reporting needs, then assess configuration, integration or custom development. Connections depend on available APIs and permissions. Access controls, migration, testing and responsibility for customer data must be agreed before implementation.

What do you need to prepare a project quote?

Start with the task you want to improve, who will use the system and the tools you already have. An intended launch window and budget range help if known, but you do not need a technical specification to start a conversation. Features, integrations, data migration, languages and support affect the scope. We discuss priorities before preparing a proposal; a fixed price or delivery date cannot be confirmed from a general enquiry alone.

Can we see relevant work before deciding?

Go Marketing does not publish client cases, app listings, project screenshots or identifying details. During a project discussion, selected relevant material may be shown privately only where the agreement and permissions allow; not every project is available to view. Where sharing is not permitted, we can explain the approach using a clearly fictional example. Read our working process and confidentiality principles.

What would you like to build or automate?

Start with one task you want to improve and the tools your team already uses. You do not need to choose a platform first. Please do not send customer records, passwords or confidential project files with your initial enquiry.
