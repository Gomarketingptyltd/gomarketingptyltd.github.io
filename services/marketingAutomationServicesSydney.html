FAQ

Common questions about marketing automation services in Sydney

Can you build an app or a customer support tool?

Yes. These builds are part of our marketing services. We first agree the business problem, users and essential features, then assess the platform and integrations. Native mobile development and app-store release requirements are confirmed during scoping rather than assumed to be included.

Can you connect our existing website and tools?

We assess your current setup before proposing a rebuild. Connections depend on the access, APIs and permissions available in each tool. Existing forms and customer records are not migrated without an agreed scope and testing plan.

Can automation still work when the business serves Chinese-speaking and English-speaking audiences?

Yes, but the message flow, routing logic and follow-up steps should still reflect different audience expectations. Good automation supports clearer bilingual communication rather than flattening it.

Should automation come before ads or before more SEO?

If the business is already getting enquiries but handling them poorly, automation can be a higher priority than adding more traffic. If the message is still unclear, website and service-page clarity often need attention first.

What is agreed before a build starts?

Features, data access, account ownership, acceptance tests, handover and ongoing support are agreed in the project scope. Hosting, software subscriptions, maintenance and third-party charges are clarified separately. Automated customer replies need clear boundaries and a route to a person.
