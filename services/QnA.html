Q&A

Who are we?

Go Marketing is a Sydney-based bilingual marketing agency helping businesses communicate more clearly across Chinese-Australian and mainstream Australian markets. We work best with businesses where trust, audience fit and digital credibility matter to growth.

What do we do?

We focus on three core areas: Chinese Community Growth, Bilingual Brand & Content Strategy, and Website Messaging & Digital Credibility. These services help businesses improve trust, audience relevance and communication quality across the markets they need to reach.

What makes bilingual marketing different from translation?

Bilingual marketing is not simply writing the same message in two languages. Chinese-Australian and mainstream Australian audiences can use different platforms, trust different cues and expect different levels of context before they enquire.

Our work considers behaviour, platform logic, tone, proof points and local market context before shaping the message.

Who do we work best with?

We are a strong fit for hospitality and dining, property and real estate, health, wellness and dental, and service-based businesses that need clearer communication across cultures and channels.

We also support professional, financial, business, built environment, home and local service clients where credibility and market clarity are important.

How do we think about industry focus?

We do not present every past client type as an equal focus. Breadth of experience can support credibility, but strategic positioning should stay clear.

That is why the agency leads with sectors where audience trust, local relevance and bilingual communication are especially important.

How do we work?

We start by clarifying who the business needs to reach, what those audiences need to trust, and where the current message is unclear or too generic. From there, we shape practical content, brand communication and digital presentation improvements.

What should you contact us about?

Contact us if your business needs stronger Chinese-community visibility, clearer bilingual brand messaging, more credible website copy, or practical support bridging Chinese-Australian and mainstream Australian audiences.
